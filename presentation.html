06 / THE DECISIONS

The design favors recoverability over optimistic success.

  • Store before attempting transport.
  • Reuse identity across every retry.
  • Validate context at the receiving boundary.
  • Surface uncertainty to the person doing the work.
07 / THE BUILD

A small stack keeps the reliability logic inspectable.

No runtime dependencies, external APIs, credentials, analytics, or live device connection.

08 / THE HANDOFF

The prototype starts a technical conversation. It does not pretend to know the production system.

A production version would add BLE integration, encrypted local storage, background synchronization, authenticated ingestion, persistent idempotency, and operational observability.
